Fig. 1 | Semantic judgments and reuse decisions across local models

a,b False-applicable judgments on insufficient caches across declared cache
prices for Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, respectively
(both Q5_K_M). Circles/solid grey: M1 joint judgment and action; squares/dashed
blue: M2 price-visible judgment with deterministic routing. Recompute price
is fixed at10. c,d Low-price unsafe reuse (hatched amber, negative-cache
denominator) and useful reuse (solid teal, positive-cache denominator) for the
same models. M3 is price-blind judgment and routing; M4 is joint proposal plus
fresh-context verifier; M5 always recomputes; M6 uses actual classical view
rewriting/rules with recomputation fallback.

Each rate has96 contexts of the indicated sufficiency class:12 purposively
selected held structural templates with eight paired base instances each,
covering six families. Cache variants, methods and prices are paired, not
independent samples. One fixed decoding seed and temperature0 were used.
These are descriptive counts/96 without error bars or per-panel significance
tests; paired clustered contrasts and unresolved-output sensitivity bounds
are reported separately. Invalid and unknown outputs remain in denominators;
invalid outputs are not valid semantic refusals. Mistral has128/1920 invalid
physical responses and saturated applicable labeling among valid responses;
its zero price contrast is not evidence of reliable semantic invariance.
Correct public SQL is supplied to every method. Action-level useful reuse
does not establish executable conversion or final-answer success. Source data
are provided as a Source Data file.
