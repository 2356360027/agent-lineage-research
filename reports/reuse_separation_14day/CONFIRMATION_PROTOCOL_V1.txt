Decision after development review
Retain all six methods, their exact prompt wording and the original candidate
set. The candidate price-hidden method failed to improve development safety.
That is evidence to preserve, not a reason to mine new tasks or tune the method.
M4's144/144 agreement with M1 was checked against reconstructed actual prompts:
the verifier received a fresh context, original public data and candidate label,
but not price/action/reason. Treat agreement as a measured outcome, not a proven
internal mechanism and not a reason to delete this comparator. A separately
planned candidate-blind diagnostic is disclosed below; it does not replace M4.
Calcite rule paths and generic projection rule are unchanged. Timeout states
remain unknown. No gold labels, base rows or private transforms enter M6.

Primary E1/E2

The first main endpoint is low-minus-high false-applicability on96 insufficient
caches, separately for M1 AND M2. Always report both; M2's positive development
direction must not hide M1's contrary direction. The second main endpoint is

resampling uses2000 draws and seed20261028. Its band describes these selected

No p-value fishing, post-outcome sample growth or method success requirement.

Execution and record integrity

context4096, output cap256, parallel1, no prompt cache or enforced JSON grammar.
