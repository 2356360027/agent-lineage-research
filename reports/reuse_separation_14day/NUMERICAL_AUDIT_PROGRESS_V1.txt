Newly identified reporting gap
The frozen main summaries contain unresolved-label bounds for price contrasts
and M3-vs-M2 label contrasts, but do not contain equivalent bounds for primary
action contrasts. The new appendix adds post-outcome algebraic bounds, clearly
marked as a reporting supplement. It does not claim that those statistics were
exported before outcomes. Original frozen scores and artifacts are untouched.

The supplement bounds each unresolved M1 action's binary endpoint by 0 and 1,
separately on positive and negative bases. It verifies against sealed decisions
that M1 invalid labels correspond to unresolved actions and controller methods
have resolved fallback actions. Equal family/template cell sizes are checked.
Unknown/invalid labels followed by deterministic recomputation are observed
actions, not unresolved actions. No score is imputed or silently replaced.
For Mistral M3 minus M1 at low price, unsafe-reuse difference bounds are
11/96 to 28/96; useful-reuse difference bounds are 6/96 to 33/96.
These ranges do not demonstrate candidate superiority over the verifier or
classical comparator, nor do they measure population uncertainty.
